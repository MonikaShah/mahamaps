Landslide Dataset

The Landslide Susceptibility Map has been prepared using topographic, geological, hydrological and environmental parameters, including slope, aspect, elevation, rainfall, drainage density, land use/land cover and lithology. The map identifies areas that are highly susceptible to landslides and supports disaster risk reduction, watershed management and sustainable planning across Maharashtra.
