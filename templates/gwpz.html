Ground Water Priority Zone Dataset

The Ground Water Priority Zone (GWPZ) map identifies areas with varying groundwater recharge potential based on integrated hydrological, geomorphological and terrain characteristics.

Watershed boundaries from HydroBASINS Levels 5–12 are provided for basin-wise exploration. Users can visualize watershed hierarchies, inspect watershed attributes and navigate from basin level to micro-watershed level.

Village selection highlights the selected village boundary while retaining the GWPZ and watershed layers for comparison.
